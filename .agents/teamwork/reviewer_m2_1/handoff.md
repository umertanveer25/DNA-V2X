## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Integrity Mandate Compliance:**
   - Prior code suffered from synthetic multipliers (10,000x scaling in streaming benchmark), decorative single-sample evaluation loops, and hardcoded numbers (`7.96` Shannon entropy).
   - In Worker M2's implementation, the loops now process real telemetry collections; entropy is calculated from observed byte and codon distributions; attack resistance is verified by executing real mutation, sybil, replay, and frequency probes against cipher instances.
   - Independent verification confirms that no shortcuts, facade objects, or hardcoded constants remain.

2. **Full-Pipeline Latency Transparency:**
   - Automotive embedded systems must account for the full ingest pipeline (feature extraction plus inference).
   - Measuring only array indexing / boolean masks produced misleading claims of 250,000 pkts/s (~4 us). By measuring and presenting both `feature_extraction_latency_us` (~1000 us) and `inference_latency_us` (~4 us), the reported streaming throughput of ~940–1,110 pkts/s represents honest, real-world edge OBU performance.

3. **VeReMi Generalization and Leakage Immunity:**
   - Shuffling vehicular trajectory packets without grouping permits future position frames of a known vehicle to contaminate the training set, causing severe artificial over-fitting.
   - Partitioning by vehicle scenario groups (`GroupKFold`) strictly isolates trajectory dynamics, guaranteeing that the test fold represents unseen vehicles. The resulting metrics (87.61% accuracy, 48.05% macro F1) represent honest empirical performance on out-of-distribution vehicular telemetry.

4. **Publication Figures Data Binding:**
   - Figures 1–6 are directly generated from `results/dna_v2x_master_results.json`.
   - Figures 7–10 are directly generated from `results/dna_v2x_50m_classification_master_results.json` and authentic IDM simulations (`MovingCarsSimulator`).

### Challenges

#### [Medium] Challenge 1: Out-of-Distribution Misbehavior Recall in VeReMi Grouped Cross-Validation
- **Assumption challenged**: That the hybrid ML classifier generalizes effortlessly to unseen vehicle scenarios.
- **Attack scenario**: On held-out vehicle scenarios (unseen trajectories in VeReMi), `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` achieves 99.86% recall on benign traffic but only 1.39% recall on malicious traffic (macro F1 = 48.05%).
- **Blast radius**: Misbehavior detection for novel vehicle types may suffer without domain adaptation or class-weighted training (`class_weight="balanced"`).
- **Mitigation**: Acknowledge this limitation honestly in the Milestone 4/5 paper discussion. It demonstrates that the evaluation is authentic, avoiding the academic flaw of over-claiming perfect cross-domain transferability. In future iterations, add class-balanced weighting or temporal sequence features.

#### [Low] Challenge 2: Dominance of Feature Extraction Latency in Real-Time Throughput
- **Assumption challenged**: That DNA-V2X classification achieves wire-speed 250,000 pkts/s.
- **Attack scenario**: An ECU receiving high-frequency BSM streams must extract 10 statistical and kinematic features per packet. Feature extraction takes ~900–1,050 us per packet, limiting throughput to ~940–1,110 pkts/s.
- **Blast radius**: If an embedded deployment only provisions compute for the ~4 us classifier inference, the buffer will overrun during high-density traffic.
- **Mitigation**: Worker M2 has appropriately remediated this by reporting both feature extraction latency and raw classification latency in Table 3 and master JSON. This ensures deployment sizing is realistic.
